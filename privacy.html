Your email. Your choice.

This notice explains how ORYN-B handles information submitted through the launch notification form and scratch-card popup on orynb.com.

What we collect

When you voluntarily subscribe, we collect your email address, your marketing consent, the time of signup, and (where selected) your product interest. We do not ask for payment information through these forms.

Why we collect it

We use this information to send ORYN-B product and launch news, including the first-drop offer when terms are confirmed. You can decline to subscribe and still browse the website. Signup does not reserve stock.

Our email service provider

We use EmailOctopus to manage subscriptions and deliver permitted marketing emails. The site may also keep a limited record of your consent to help manage signups and removal requests.

Unsubscribe and deletion

Every marketing email will provide an unsubscribe link. If you want to ask about your information or request deletion, reply to a message sent by ORYN-B. We do not sell the email addresses collected through these forms.

Updates

We may update this notice before launch as our services and legal information are finalized. The current version describes only the launch-list signup experience.
